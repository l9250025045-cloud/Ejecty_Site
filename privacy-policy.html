2. What the Service Does

The Service is a mobile application designed to help users play specific sound frequencies, tones, sound patterns, and/or vibration-like audio signals that may assist in cleaning a device speaker from water, dust, debris, or other particles.

The Service is primarily designed to play sound through your device speaker. Unless a specific feature clearly states otherwise and you grant the relevant device permission, the Service does not need access to your microphone, camera, photos, contacts, precise location, or files.

The Service does not perform a certified technical diagnosis of your device and does not determine whether your speaker is physically damaged.
