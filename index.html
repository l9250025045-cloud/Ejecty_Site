Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about Ejecty, subscriptions, and how the app works.

Ejecty plays specially tuned sound frequencies and low-frequency vibrations through your speaker. These sound waves help push out trapped water, dust, and debris. Just pick a mode, press play, and let the sound do the work.

Yes. Ejecty only plays sound through your device's speaker — it doesn't use any liquids, tools, or physical contact. We recommend starting at a lower volume and increasing it gradually. Ejecty does not perform a certified technical diagnosis and cannot determine whether a speaker is physically damaged.

The Water Eject mode plays low-frequency tones designed to vibrate water out of the speaker grille. Results depend on your device and how much liquid is trapped. For best results, hold the phone with the speaker facing down and run the mode a few times.

Ejecty includes free features plus an optional subscription that unlocks all cleaning modes, the Frequency Generator, and the dB Meter without limits. The exact price and billing period are always shown in the app before you confirm a purchase.

Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID. On your iPhone, open Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → select Ejecty → Cancel Subscription. Your access stays active until the end of the current billing period.

Purchases are handled by the App Store. To request a refund, visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID, find Ejecty in your purchase history, and follow the steps. Refund decisions are made by Apple.
